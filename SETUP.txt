360 PHARMACY v5 - ONE SUPABASE AUTH LOGIN

READ README.md for exact setup and limitations.

Do not run both SQL files.
  - Brand-new empty Supabase project: supabase/CREATE_NEW_DATABASE.sql
  - Existing empty v4 Pharmacy360 schema: supabase/UPGRADE_EMPTY_V4_TO_AUTH.sql

Supabase Auth Dashboard:
  Confirm email ON
  Confirmation email template includes {{ .Token }} for customer OTP
  Reset password email template includes {{ .ConfirmationURL }}; no invite template needed for new staff
  Site URL / Redirect URL include http://127.0.0.1:8000/login or your deployment URL
  Configure custom SMTP for sending real account emails

Copy server/.env.example -> server/.env and fill DATABASE_URL, SUPABASE_URL,
SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY, SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY, PUBLIC_SITE_URL,
APP_SECRET and ADMIN_SETUP_TOKEN. Never put secret keys in client code.

Windows:
  INSTALL_DEPENDENCIES.bat
  START_SYSTEM.bat
  Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/login

Create the first Admin privately through Supabase Authentication > Users; assign its admin role in public.profiles.
Admin creates staff directly under Staff Accounts with role, email and initial password. No invitation email is sent; staff sign in on the shared login page.
No role selection on login, no separate staff login page, no demo accounts.
